# Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is a popular Netflix original series created by *The Duffer Brothers*.  
It mixes **science fiction**, **horror**, and **80s nostalgia** — set in the small town of *Hawkins, Indiana*.

---

## 🧠 Main Plot
A young boy named **Will Byers** mysteriously disappears.  
His friends and family uncover **a secret government experiment** and a **parallel dimension** called the *Upside Down*.

---

## 🎬 Main Characters
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** – A girl with psychokinetic powers  
- **Mike Wheeler** – Will’s loyal best friend  
- **Jim Hopper** – The town’s police chief  
- **Joyce Byers** – Will’s determined mother  
- **Dustin Henderson** – The funny and smart member of the group  

---

## 💬 My Thoughts
> "Friends don't lie." — Eleven  
I like *Stranger Things* because of its mysterious story, cool powers, and strong friendship themes.
